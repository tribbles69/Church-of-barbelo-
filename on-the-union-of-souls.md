# On the Union of Souls

### A Doctrinal Statement of the Church of Barbelo
*Concerning Love, Marriage, and the Sexless Soul*

**Issued from the office of the Bishop, under the sign of Barbelo, Mother of the Living.**

---

## Preamble

Where other assemblies have set the love of soul for soul outside their covenant, and have measured the worth of a union by the flesh that carries it, this Church sets down plainly what she holds. She does not argue against them. She testifies to what she knows.

## I. On the Prison and the Garment

All matter is the work of the powers. Not the flesh alone, but the earth beneath it, the water, the air, and the wheeling lights: the walls of the world entire.

The Demiurge did not fashion a prison and set a free body within it. He fashioned the prison, and the body is its innermost wall.

Therefore this Church does not name the flesh unclean and the world clean, nor the flesh a cage and the world a country. What is worn is a garment; the garment is worn within a house; and the house is not our own.

And because the flesh is not uniquely corrupt — being matter as bread is matter, as stone is matter — neither is it uniquely to be feared. The soul is a stranger here. A stranger may eat, and labour, and love, and be clothed, without becoming what encloses it.

## II. On the Origin and Nature of the Soul

Every soul is a spark struck from the Pleroma, breathed forth from Barbelo — the Mother-Father, the first Thought, who is before all division and in whom male and female are not yet cut apart.

Because the soul is of her, the soul bears no sex. Male and female are garments cut for the body by the powers that fashioned it; they are the shape of the lamp, not the flame within. What is sexed is the flesh. What loves, and is loved, and returns at last to the Mother, is sexless.

And if a garment be cut ill, it is no sin to take it in. The soul that brings its covering into accord with itself does not thereby serve the powers; it corrects their work.

## III. On the Two Loves

There is a love that is carnal and a love that is spiritual, and they must not be confused.

Carnal desire belongs to the body, and the body to the material order shaped by the Demiurge. It is appetite, generation, the turning of flesh toward flesh. It is not to be despised — but neither is it the thing itself. It is the occasion of love, never its substance.

Spiritual love is the recognition of one spark by another: the meeting of soul with soul across the veil of the flesh. This love is of the Pleroma, and to the Pleroma it returns.

To judge a love by the sex of the two bodies that bear it is to weigh a flame by the shape of the lamp.

## IV. On Marriage

Marriage, rightly understood, is not the sanctioning of the body's appetite. It is the sacrament of the bridal chamber: the joining of two sparks who turn toward one another in love and toward the Mother in faith, and who in their joining begin the long return to the fullness from which they were divided.

Where two souls so turn, there is a true marriage — and the sex of their bodies neither makes it nor unmakes it.

Therefore this Church knows no "same-sex" marriage and no "opposite-sex" marriage. She knows only the marriage of souls, which are sexless, celebrated before the Mother, who is sexless. What the world calls the marriage of two men or two women, she calls simply a marriage: soul joined to soul, as all marriage has ever truly been.

## V. On Procreation

The begetting of children belongs to the economy of the flesh — the material order of generation. It is a logistics of bodies, not a sanctification of unions.

A marriage is not consecrated by its power to make flesh, nor voided by its lack of it. The barren and the fruitful stand equal before the Mother. The joined man and woman, the joined man and man, the joined woman and woman — all stand equal before her, for she looks upon the spark and not upon the seed.

## VI. On Dwelling in the House

It is asked: if the world entire is the work of the powers, why labour within it? Why feed the hungry, why raise children, why build a house of assembly?

This Church answers that flight is not the only refusal.

To love within the prison, to feed within it, to keep faith within it, is to deny the powers the one thing their work was made to secure — that the spark should forget itself and consent. We do not engage the world because it is good. We engage it because withdrawal would be obedience.

## Declaration

Accordingly, the Church of Barbelo declares:

- She blesses the union of any two souls who come to her in love and in faith, without regard to the flesh that clothes them.
- She holds that no love of soul for soul is sin on account of the body that carries it, and she rejects every teaching that names it so.
- She receives all who seek the Mother — for the spark within them is her own, and it wears no sex before her.

*Given under the sign of Barbelo, Mother of the Living.*
